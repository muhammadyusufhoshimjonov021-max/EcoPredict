# EcoPredict
### AI for a Cleaner Future

**Building AI course project**

## Summary

EcoPredict is a proposed AI project that aims to predict air quality in Uzbekistan. It will use environmental and weather data to estimate pollution levels and help people make more informed decisions.

## Background

Air pollution is an important environmental challenge. Poor air quality can affect human health, the environment, and everyday life.

EcoPredict aims to address this problem by using artificial intelligence to predict air pollution levels.

## How is it used?

The project is planned as a simple website where users can:

- Select a city in Uzbekistan.
- View available air quality information.
- See predicted pollution levels.
- Learn about environmental conditions.

The first development stage will focus on Tashkent. Later, the project could expand to other cities in Uzbekistan.

## Data sources and AI methods

The project may use environmental datasets containing:

- PM2.5 and PM10 measurements.
- Temperature and humidity.
- Wind speed and direction.
- Historical air quality measurements.

The planned AI methods include:

- **Linear Regression:** a simple starting method for predicting pollution levels.
- **Random Forest:** a possible method for identifying more complex patterns.

Potential data sources will include publicly available air quality and weather datasets. Their reliability and licenses will be checked before use.

## Challenges

Some expected challenges are:

- Finding reliable and complete environmental data.
- Handling missing or inaccurate measurements.
- Accounting for seasonal and weather-related changes.
- Making predictions understandable to ordinary users.
- Avoiding misleading predictions.

## What next?

1. Find reliable air quality and weather datasets.
2. Learn Python for data analysis.
3. Build a basic prediction model.
4. Test the model using historical data.
5. Develop a simple website.
6. Expand the project to more cities in Uzbekistan.
